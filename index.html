The gathering

Come celebrate at the farm for Uma's 29th — an open afternoon of connection, trading , eating, and being outside together. Good food, good people, wide open land.

Make something to trade — optional

Bring something to trade at the market! Bring 8–10 of whatever you make, individually packaged at sample size. Leave with 8-10 new items! Some ideas:

  • Baked goods or preserves
  • Handmade soap or candles
  • Art, prints, or pressed flowers
  • Knitted or sewn things
  • Dried herbs or garden goods
  • Anything made with care

We'll Eat

Burgers Veggie burger Hot dogs Corn Snacks

The afternoon

Birthday blessing, yard games, cold plunge, a shared meal, and good music. Dress for the outdoors. Come for a fruitful afternoon celebrating the wonderful Ms. Uma and the end of summer.
